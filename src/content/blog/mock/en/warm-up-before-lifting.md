A warm-up does not need to be long. It needs to raise body temperature a little and rehearse the movements that are about to be loaded.

## 1. General: 3–5 minutes

Light cardio — a bike, a rower, brisk walking — just enough to feel warmer.

## 2. Movement prep: 2–3 minutes

A few dynamic movements related to the session: leg swings and bodyweight squats before a leg day, band pull-aparts and arm circles before an upper-body day.

## 3. Ramp-up sets

For the first big lift, a few lighter sets climbing towards the working weight, for example:

1. Empty bar × 8–10
2. About 50% × 5
3. About 70% × 3
4. About 85% × 1–2

These sets are preparation, not training volume — keep them easy, and do not count them as working sets.

## Skip the long static stretching beforehand

Long static holds right before heavy lifting are not needed for most people; they fit better after the session or on a separate day.
