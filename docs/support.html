Cancelling a subscription

A subscription bought through the App Store is managed by Apple, and is cancelled in the Apple ID settings rather than inside Gymon.

  • On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Gymon → Cancel Subscription.

Manage subscriptions at Apple

Deleting the account

The account is deleted from inside the app:

  1. Open the “Profile” tab and tap the “Settings” (gear) button at the top of the screen.
  2. Tap “Delete account”.
  3. The screen that opens lists what will and will not be deleted. Tap “Delete account”, then confirm with “Delete”.

Deleting the account permanently removes everything stored in it. This can't be undone, and the data can't be recovered afterwards.

What will be deleted

  • Account details and email address
  • Profile and questionnaire answers
  • Workout plans, logged workouts and sets
  • Weight measurements and personal notes
  • Conversations with the coach
  • Preferences, favourites and submitted reports

What is not deleted

  • Data in Apple Health stays in the Health app. Gymon does not store it.
  • Messages and photos already sent to OpenAI for processing may be kept there for up to 30 days, under OpenAI's policy.
  • A subscription bought through the App Store is not cancelled by deleting the account. Subscriptions are cancelled in App Store settings.

For an account created with Apple, Apple will ask for confirmation so the link between Apple and the account can be removed.
