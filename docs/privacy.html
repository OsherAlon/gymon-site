Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Introduction

Gymon is a personal workout tracking application. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use Gymon, for what purposes, and what rights you have regarding this information. Gymon may be used only by those aged 16 or over, and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under that age; an account found to have been opened in breach of this will be deleted, together with the data linked to it.

Who is responsible for your data

Gymon is operated as a private business by Osher Alon, who is the controller responsible for the personal data processed in the app. For any question or request regarding this policy or the exercise of your rights over your personal data, you may contact us at gymon.official@gmail.com.

The personal data we collect

The application collects only the following data:

Registration details

First name, last name, and email address. The application does not use passwords. You can open an account and sign in in one of two ways, as you prefer: a one-time code sent to an email address you give the app directly, or signing in with an existing Apple or Google account. When you sign in through an identity provider, that provider gives Gymon an email address, and in the case of Google also the name on the account; no password is passed on, and Gymon has no access to your account with that provider. A name not supplied this way can be filled in and edited on the profile screen.

Relay addresses when signing in with Apple

When signing in with an Apple account you can choose "Hide My Email". If you do, Apple does not give Gymon your personal email address but a unique relay address ending in privaterelay.appleid.com, and forwards messages sent to it on to your personal inbox. That relay address is what Gymon stores, and it identifies the account. This is how the service is designed to work, not a fault. Please note that severing the link between Gymon and your Apple account from your device settings stops the relay working — from that point on, messages from Gymon will no longer arrive. To keep access uninterrupted, we recommend contacting us at the address in the Contact section to set an alternative address before doing so.

App usage data

The workouts you build (exercises, sets, planned reps, order), your actual workout performance data (weights, reps, and times logged), and any personal notes you choose to add about specific exercises. Problem reports sent from within the app are also stored: the content of the report, and the type and version of the device's operating system. In addition, and only if a separate consent has been turned on for it — which is off by default — conversations with Gymon are saved in the account: everything written and chosen in them, including health information if written (such as pain, injury or weight). A saved conversation is deleted automatically 90 days after its last message; each conversation can be deleted individually from within the app, and turning the consent off in Settings deletes all saved conversations. In addition, where a subscription is purchased or a purchase is attempted, subscription and purchase data is received — as described in the "Payments and subscriptions" section below.

Profile and plan personalisation data

The details provided in the questionnaire in order to build a personalised plan: gender, date of birth, height, training goal, experience level, training location and available equipment, and weekly frequency. Body weight over time and a target weight are also stored, where entered. In addition, and entirely voluntarily, areas of the body where there is a limitation or difficulty, and movements best avoided, may be provided so that the plan can be adapted. Height, weight, target weight, and the limitations and movements provided are health data: in the questionnaire they are collected only after explicit consent, given on a dedicated screen before those questions, and each of those questions can be skipped. They are used only to adapt the plan and track progress, and are not sent to any outside party. All of them can be deleted at any time in Settings, and the deletion is recorded as a withdrawal of consent; height and current weight can also be updated there.

Data from your device's health app

You may choose to connect Gymon to your device's health app (Apple Health). The connection is read-only and is made only after your explicit, separate consent — both on a dedicated screen in the app and in the operating system's own permission dialog. The data read is: steps, logged workouts, heart rate (including resting heart rate), active energy, and walking and running distance. This constitutes health data. It stays on your device, is used to display your activity and adapt your plan, and is not sent to Gymon's servers or shared with any third party. Declining does not prevent you from using the app, and you can revoke access at any time in your device settings, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

What we do not collect

The application does not collect location data, contacts access, or payment method details (such as a card number) — purchases are made with Apple, as described in the "Payments and subscriptions" section below — and does not store photos: a photo chosen in the equipment-identification feature is sent for processing only, as described in the "Artificial intelligence: chatting with Gymon and identifying equipment from a photo" section below. A connection to your device's health app is made only at your initiative and with your explicit consent, as described in "Data from your device's health app" above; without it, no data is read from it. Beyond that, the application does not connect to other external health devices or services.

Why we collect this information

  • Registration details are collected to identify you, authenticate you when logging in, and contact you if needed. Where you sign in through an identity provider, the details received from that provider are used for those same purposes only.
  • App usage data is collected to provide the service itself: storing your workout history, displaying it to you, and tracking your progress over time. Problem reports are used only to find and fix faults in the app. Saved conversations are used only to display them back: they are not re-sent to OpenAI and are not used for analysis or any other purpose. Subscription data is used to grant access to the features included in the subscription, to show the subscription status on the settings screen, and to restore purchases.
  • Profile data is collected in order to build a personalised training plan and adapt it over time. A physical limitation you provide is used solely to leave exercises out of the plan.

Legal basis for processing

Personal data is processed on the basis of the consent you give when registering for the service, and to the extent necessary to provide the service you requested — without your registration details and usage data, we cannot provide you with the service. Choosing to sign in through an identity provider is a separate, voluntary consent to that particular transfer, and an alternative that does not involve one is available. With respect to health data — a physical limitation you have provided — processing is based on explicit, separate consent, given separately from general consent and withdrawable at any time. Use of the Gymon chat feature, which involves transferring data to OpenAI outside Israel and the European Union, is based on explicit, separate consent — including with respect to any health data included in the conversation — which is not a condition of using the app and can be withdrawn at any time. Saving conversations in the account is based on a further separate consent, off by default, which is not a condition of using the chat or the app, and withdrawing it deletes the saved conversations. A photo is sent for equipment identification only at the user's request, each time anew and for the purpose of providing the requested feature, and doing so is not a condition of using the app. Processing subscription and purchase data, including passing it to RevenueCat, is necessary to perform the subscription the user chose to take out, to grant the access purchased, and to check entitlement to the app's features; for users who have not purchased a subscription, only the account identifier and the technical data needed for that check are passed to RevenueCat.

Sharing data with third parties

Your personal data is stored and processed using Supabase, a technology infrastructure provider (database, authentication, and hosting services), whose relevant servers for this service are located in Zurich, Switzerland. Switzerland is not a member of the European Union, but the European Union has recognised its level of data protection as adequate, in an adequacy decision of the European Commission. Supabase processes this data solely to provide these technical services to the app's operator, and does not use it for any other purpose. In addition, when the chat feature is used, and subject to separate consent, and when the feature for identifying equipment from a photo is used, data is sent to OpenAI, as described in the "Artificial intelligence: chatting with Gymon and identifying equipment from a photo" section below. The app's emails, including one-time sign-in codes, are sent through Google's email service (Gmail), which for that purpose receives the recipient's address and the content of the message. To manage subscriptions, Gymon uses a service from RevenueCat, Inc. — an external provider that processes the data in the United States and acts as a processor on behalf of the app's operator — as described in the "Payments and subscriptions" section below.

In addition, you may choose to sign in with an existing Apple or Google account. Making that choice necessarily involves the provider in question: it verifies your identity and gives Gymon a signed identity token along with the details described under Registration details above, and in doing so learns that Gymon is being used and when a sign-in took place. Gymon does not pass workout data, profile data, or health data to these providers, and does not use them for advertising or measurement. Using this option is not required — signing in with a one-time code by email remains fully available and involves no provider other than Supabase. Apple's and Google's own use of data is governed by their privacy policies, which are outside the app operator's control.

As of the date of this policy's last update, the application does not use advertising or tracking services, and does not use analytics services to measure how the app is used; accordingly, it does not share personal data with providers of this kind. RevenueCat provides the app's operator with aggregate subscription and revenue reports based on purchase data only. Payments and their processing are described in the Payments and subscriptions section below.

Payments and subscriptions

Gymon offers an auto-renewing subscription (Gymon Premium), yearly or monthly, with a free trial period for those eligible for it. Purchase and payment take place solely through Apple's in-app purchase system (the App Store): Apple charges the payment and holds the payment method details, and Gymon does not receive or store any card number, billing details or billing address.

To manage subscription entitlement, Gymon uses a service from RevenueCat, Inc., which acts as a processor on behalf of the app's operator and stores the data on servers in the United States. RevenueCat receives: the Gymon account identifier (an internal user ID, without name or email address); purchase data from Apple — the purchase receipt, the product purchased, the purchase, renewal and expiry dates, the trial period, and the subscription status; and technical data that RevenueCat's software library passes on when contacting its servers, such as device type and operating system, the time of last use, and the IP address of the device, which contacts RevenueCat directly. The account identifier and the technical data are passed on at every sign-in on an Apple device, even without a purchase, in order to check entitlement; purchase data is passed on only when a purchase or a restore takes place. RevenueCat processes this data to verify the purchase with Apple, determine entitlement and provide aggregate reports to the app's operator, as committed in its data processing addendum, which incorporates the European Union's Standard Contractual Clauses. From RevenueCat, Gymon receives only the subscription status — the plan type, whether it is in a trial, whether it renews and until when it is valid — in order to grant access and show that status on the settings screen. Apple processes purchase details as an independent party under its own privacy policy, which is outside the app operator's control.

Please note that deleting your Gymon account does not cancel an active subscription with Apple. A subscription is cancelled in the device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or from the app's Settings → Manage subscription, and it is recommended to cancel before deleting the account.

Artificial intelligence: chatting with Gymon and identifying equipment from a photo

The in-app chat feature uses a language model from OpenAI — an external provider that processes the data in the United States — to understand text typed into the chat and to phrase a reply. Sending a typed message to OpenAI requires explicit, separate consent, given by tapping a button in the chat's opening message, which is not a condition of using the app. The consent is recorded in the account, together with its time and the version of the wording shown, and Gymon's servers do not pass any message to OpenAI unless it has been given, or after it has been withdrawn. Without consent nothing is sent to OpenAI, and the chat keeps working with buttons and prepared replies. The consent can be withdrawn at any time in Settings; from that moment no further data is sent, and what has already been sent is kept by OpenAI as described below. Each time a typed message is sent, the following is sent to OpenAI: the typed text; up to the last six messages in the conversation, including answers chosen in it with buttons — which may include sensitive areas of the body; the question shown at that moment and the app language; answers already given in that conversation about the requested workout; and, from the profile — training goal, experience level, training location, weekly frequency and preferred session length. Typed text is sent as written, so anything written in it — including health information such as pain or injury, weight, height or date of birth — reaches OpenAI.

The feature for identifying equipment from a photo, available while building a workout, also uses a model from OpenAI. When a photo is chosen — taken with the camera or selected from the photo library, and only after access is granted through the operating system's permission prompt — the photo is sent, compressed, to OpenAI to identify the equipment shown in it, and the result is used to suggest exercises from Gymon's exercise catalogue. No name, email address, user ID or profile data is sent with the photo, and the photo is not stored on Gymon's servers. Since everything visible in the photo reaches OpenAI, it is recommended to photograph the equipment only and to avoid photographing people, faces or identifying details. Using the feature is optional and is not a condition of using the app; exercises can also be added by searching.

What is not sent in the chat: name, email address, user ID, workout history, and, from the profile — age, height, weight and the stored physical limitation. For both features, the request is made from Gymon's servers, not directly from the device, so the device's IP address is not passed to OpenAI. The transfer takes place under OpenAI's data processing addendum, which incorporates the European Union's Standard Contractual Clauses. OpenAI commits not to use data sent through its API to train or improve its models, and retains it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, unless the law requires longer retention. Replies and exercise suggestions are generated automatically and may be wrong; they are not reviewed by a medical professional and are not medical advice or a diagnosis.

Retention period

Your personal data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You can delete your account at any time from within the app, under Settings → Delete account. Conversations with Gymon, where saving them was chosen, are deleted automatically 90 days after their last message, or earlier when deleted from within the app. The record of consents to the AI features (given or withdrawn, time and wording version) is kept while the account is active, in order to demonstrate the consent. Account deletion is immediate and final, and covers all data linked to the account. Data already sent to OpenAI for processing may be kept there for up to 30 days under OpenAI's own policy, which is outside Gymon's control. Purchase records are kept by Apple under its own policy. When the account is deleted, the link between the subscription and the Gymon account is removed, but the subscription record at RevenueCat, which is linked to the account identifier only, is not deleted automatically; its deletion can be requested by contacting us at the address in the Contact section, and we will have it deleted with RevenueCat.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — to obtain information about the personal data held about you.
  • Rectification — to request correction of inaccurate or outdated data.
  • Erasure — to request deletion of your personal data ("the right to be forgotten").
  • Portability — to receive your personal data in a structured, transferable format.
  • Objection and restriction — to object to certain processing of your data, or request that it be restricted.
  • Withdrawal of consent — to withdraw your consent to processing at any time, including explicit consent to the processing of health data.
  • Complaint — to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority, if you believe your rights have been violated.

How to exercise these rights today: account deletion is done within the app, under Settings → Delete account. The other rights listed above are exercised by contacting us directly by email.

How we keep your data secure

Access to your personal data on Supabase's servers is restricted to you alone, via a Row Level Security mechanism that operates based on the identity of the authenticated user. All communication between the app and the servers is encrypted.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. A material update will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this document, and where appropriate, we will also notify you within the app.

Contact
