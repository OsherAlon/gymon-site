Gymon

Gymon is an app for tracking personal workouts.

Workout log

Log a set in seconds

Weight and reps for each set, with last time's numbers already filled in. Mark a set as done and the rest timer starts.

Chat with Gymon

Stuck? Ask Gymon

Ask about a lift that won't budge and get practical ideas for the next workout.

Your plan

A week built around you

Your goal, how often you train and where, turned into a typical week of workouts.

Profile

Steps and weight in one place

Your weekly step average, the days you hit your step goal, and your weight against your target.

Workout summary

Every workout, wrapped up

When you finish: the time, the exercises completed, the total weight lifted and every set.

What's inside Gymon

A quick look at what the app does.

A plan built for you

Short questionnaire

Answer a few questions about your goal, experience, equipment and training days, and get a weekly plan with sets, reps and rest.

Last time's numbers, right there

Workout log

Every set shows the weight and reps from the last run of the same workout, so the next small step is clear.

A rest timer that starts itself

Workout log

Mark a set as done and the rest timer starts. Timed exercises like running get a stopwatch instead.

Build a workout in a chat

Chat with Gymon

Say what you want to train, how much time you have and what to avoid, and Gymon builds the workout with you.

Your best set, per exercise

History

See past sessions of each exercise, the best set in each, and how your volume moves over time.

Fully in Hebrew, right to left

Language

Every screen works in Hebrew from right to left, and in English.

How-to for every exercise

Exercise library

Each exercise in the library comes with instructions on how to do it, in Hebrew too.

Steps from Apple Health

Optional

With your permission, Gymon reads steps and workouts from Health. It never writes to Health, and the data stays on your phone.
