How many sets a week? A practical look at training volume

Volume — the number of hard sets per muscle per week — is one of the most useful dials in a training plan. A simple way to set it and adjust it.

by Gymon Team Sep 21, 2026 6 min read

Training volume is usually counted as hard sets per muscle group per week: sets taken reasonably close to failure, not warm-ups.

A starting range

Many lifters do well somewhere around 10 to 20 hard sets per muscle group per week, spread over two or more sessions. Newer lifters often progress on the lower end of that range; more experienced lifters may need more.

Spread it out

Twelve sets for the chest are easier to do well as two sessions of six than as one session of twelve. Later sets in a long session tend to be lower quality.

Adjust from what the log shows

  • Performance climbing and recovery good → the current volume is working.
  • Performance flat for several weeks, recovery good → consider adding a set or two.
  • Performance dropping and soreness lingering → consider removing a few sets.

The numbers above are a starting point, not a rule. What a training log shows over a few weeks is a better guide than any table.

General information only, not medical advice. Anyone with a medical condition, an injury or pain should consult a qualified professional before starting or changing a training routine.
